Project Information

Toward Agentic AI-powered NextG Network with Integrated Sensing and Communication for IoT-based Healthcare

Lead: Rutgers University
Collaborative Partner: Temple University

“Toward Agentic AI-powered NextG Network with Integrated Sensing and Communication for IoT-based Healthcare” is an NSF VINES Track 1 project led by Rutgers University in collaboration with Temple University. The project aims to design a novel hierarchical agentic AI-enabled wireless network architecture to support continuous, personalized “healthcare on the go.” AI-driven healthcare requires real-time inference on massive multimodal data streams, creating stringent requirements for latency, bandwidth, reliability, and sensing accuracy that today’s best-effort wireless networks cannot adequately support.

Supported by the National Science Foundation · Awards 2548782 and 2548783
