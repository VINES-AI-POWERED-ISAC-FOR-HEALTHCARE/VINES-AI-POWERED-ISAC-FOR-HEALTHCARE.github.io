Project Information

Toward Agentic AI-powered NextG Network with Integrated Sensing and Communication for IoT-based Healthcare

Lead: Rutgers University
Collaborative Partner: Temple University

This NSF VINES project brings together Rutgers University and Temple University to develop an agentic AI-powered NextG wireless network that integrates sensing and communication for IoT-based healthcare. The project aims to enable continuous, proactive “healthcare on the go,” supporting daily activity monitoring, dangerous event detection, vital sign tracking, and sleep quality assessment. Its hierarchical architecture coordinates smart IoT devices, AI-enabled routers, and intelligent edge servers to connect sensing and local inference with healthcare-aware routing and network resource management. By combining robust multimodal sensing with Information-Centric Networking and efficient AI deployment, the team seeks to prioritize critical healthcare alerts, adapt to user mobility and changing environments, and reduce communication delays and energy use. The project will develop reusable prototypes, algorithms, and datasets to support future research in intelligent wireless healthcare systems.

Supported by the National Science Foundation · Awards 2548782 and 2548783
